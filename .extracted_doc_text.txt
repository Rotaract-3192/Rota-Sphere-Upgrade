The final VIBE concept

🪙 COINS

Flow through the event.

Users earn them → spend them → spent coins contribute to zones.

⭐ XP

Measures engagement.

Activities, stalls, photos, games and quests give XP.

🌊 ZONES

Compete against each other.

Arnava vs Taranaga vs Sagara vs Pravaha vs Samudhra vs Varuna.

👤 USERS

Compete individually.

Highest verified XP wins.

📸 STALLS

Become interactive experiences, not just booths.

🎮 GAMES

Give users another way to participate, earn XP and potentially earn coins.

📊 ZONAL STATS

Show exactly how each zone performed and give the organizers meaningful post-event data.

So the overall philosophy becomes:

🪙 Spend your VIBE. ⭐ Earn your XP. 🌊 Support your zone. 🎮 Play the event. 📸 Capture the experience. 🏆 Become the VIBE Champion.
